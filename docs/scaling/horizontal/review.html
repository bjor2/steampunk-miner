    Alternatives · steampunk-miner horizontal scaling · Schedule C approved

    Schedule C approved: act arcs + artefact drip through planet 40

    Before Schedule C the horizontal cache listed 20 features: 14 of them landed on planet 1, and nothing new arrived on planets 16–40. The resolution of What type and direction of scalability does the game commit to? asks for a schedule of what is new on every planet of a 30–40 planet campaign. This page compared three ways to fill that schedule. Bjor auto-approved option C (act arcs + artefact drip) on 2026-10-05 (“This should always auto approve.”). The approved schedule is locked in stats.json (#80, 56 rows, cumulative 61 at planet 40). Six campaign rows are shipped as of T3 (#127): refinery_bay, auto_guns, tunnel_wrecker, blasting_charges, heat_lava, refractory_lining. #162 (Game Director, 6 Oct): research_lab moves from P24 to P15 as the platform building where milestone combos are researched (endless combos too), and P24 is left open; side_drills, shields, grav_anchor, buoyancy_tanks and escape_thrusters stay at their planets as tech-tree nodes (unlockVia: "tech_tree", treeLane per #160). #153 (Gameplay & Vehicle Designer, 6 Oct): remote_detonator joins P22 as its own Upgrade row; dynamite sizes 2–10 extend blasting_charges and get no rows. A/B remain below for reference only.

    2. Three schedule strategies

    All three keep the seeded vision items and the slice as they are. They differ in how the new beats are spaced and themed. Option C is approved; A and B are kept for comparison.

    A

    Steady drip: one new feature every two planets

    Low content

    Keep the seeded vision items and add one feature from a backlog on every even planet that has none (4, 14, 16 … 40).

    • Smallest content bill (15 new features)
    • Easy to test: one schedule rule, a gap check in CI
    • Closes the planet 16–40 cliff
    • No themes: features arrive in backlog order, not because the planet asks for them
    • No climaxes; the vehicle changes shape at random times
    • Ends at 35, the lowest of the three

    Reversible
    Yes, it is only a schedule
    B

    Milestone clusters every fifth planet

    Medium content

    Pull the seeded vision items onto milestone planets (5, 10, 15) and land three or four new features together on every fifth planet, each cluster with a visible vehicle or platform change.

    • Big, memorable moments; the vehicle visibly transforms at each milestone (design §10)
    • Simple to communicate: “every fifth planet is an upgrade planet”
    • Clusters bundle art and testing work
    • Four empty planets between clusters, about three hours at slice pace
    • Three to four systems learned at once on milestone planets
    • Heat would move from planet 8 to 10, wagons from 12 to 10

    C

    Act arcs with an artefact drip

    ApprovedMost content

    Five acts, each opened by a planet archetype that arrives with its own enemy and its own casing answer, closed by a boss, story or signature beat. Artefact sets (two new picks) fill the gaps cheaply, because the per-planet cache already exists after slice 2.

    • Every beat has a reason: the planet creates a problem, the next unlock answers it
    • Uses what slice 2 builds: casing grades get lining types, the artefact cache gets a growing pool
    • Covers all of Bjor’s categories in every act: items, upgrades, merchants, enemies, features
    • Answers both open #35 questions (casing and artefacts across the campaign)
    • Biggest bill: 38 new features, though 13 of them are artefact picks or lining types on existing systems
    • Needs the archetype hook (1.3) by planet 8
    • Act themes lock some ordering; moving heat later moves its enemy and lining too

    3. Approved schedule (option C), act by act

    All acts below mirror stats.json (refreshed for #162: statuses from #127, research lab at P15, tech-tree node badges, #78 folds and #80 fills; and for #153: remote detonator at P22). Cadence rule: never more than two planets (roughly 1.5 hours at slice pace) without a new capability; an archetype, vehicle change or boss every three to four planets; artefact picks and enemy behaviours in between.

    4. Campaign rules for casing, artefacts and guns

    The second-slice map leaves “full campaign horizontal schedule updates for casing and artefacts” under Not yet specified. These three rules close it; each can be approved on its own.

    4.1

    Casing: grade stays vertical, lining types are the horizontal beats

    Moderate
    Planet 1: standard lining, casingGrade ≥ band (slice 2 rule, unchanged)
    ↓
    Planet 8: refractory lining for lava bands
    ↓
    Planet 17: insulated lining against refreezing ice
    ↓
    Planet 25: grounded lining, safe corridors through storms

    Proposal. Casing grade keeps scaling by formula (vertical); each new archetype adds one lining type the player must choose and pay for (horizontal). The vehicle lays one type at a time, so switching is a decision.

    Open point. Whether re-lining an existing tunnel with a new type costs anything; the slice’s relining rule suggests no.

    4.2

    Artefacts: one cache per planet, pool grows by two every act

    Cheap

    Proposal. Keep the slice rule (one cache per planet, exclusive 3-pick, no pure stat bumps) and add two act-themed picks at planets 5, 11, 19, 27 and 35. Each cache offers at least one pick the player has never been offered while any remain.

    Decision needed. What re-picking an owned artefact does once the pool is exhausted; suggestion: it upgrades that artefact (a vertical tail), which keeps every cache worth opening.

    4.3

    Move automated guns from planet 15 to planet 4

    Medium
    Automatic guns could appear around the third major planet or progression milestone, depending on pacing. — docs/design.md §11

    Problem. The seed puts auto_guns at 15, far from the design’s hint, while planets 3–5 have nothing.

    Proposal. Planet 4: the first planet after the refinery bay, early enough to change how the player handles side hits for the rest of the campaign. Planet 15 gets the core guardian instead.
