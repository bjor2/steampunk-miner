    Vertical curves: drill upgrades outrun income by 18x at planet 40

    Drill power keeps pace with rock hardness, but its price climbs faster than ore value, so each upgrade costs more ore every planet. Casing is listed as a vertical track yet has no curve, and it is the obvious sink for the 22-62 minutes slice 2 is short of its pacing target. Comment on each finding where you disagree or know the intent, then copy all comments back into the chat.

    1.1

    drill_power next cost climbs 3.63x per planet

    High

    Problem. Six drill levels per planet at r = 1.24 make the next upgrade 3.63x dearer each planet, from 787 at planet 1 to 5.71e24 at planet 40.

    Impact. Ore value only grows 3.38x per planet, so the curve is about 7.5% per planet steeper than income.

    1.2

    Drill power at end of planet tracks hardness exactly

    Low

    Finding. The drill feels the same on every planet: power is pure maintenance against hardness, never felt growth.

    Impact. That is fine for the formula, but it means any sense of getting stronger must come from horizontal features, not this track.

    1.3

    Ore needed per drill upgrade drifts from 16 to 281

    High

    Impact. The pacing bot already shows core time jumping from about 50 to 69 minutes at planets 7-8; at this drift planet 40 needs 18x the mining per upgrade of planet 1.

    Suggested change. Either set r so that r^6 matches 1.5^3 (r = 1.225) for a flat ratio, or keep the drift on purpose and state the per-planet time budget it implies.

    1.4

    Travel fee is a stable 1.2x band-5 ore value

    Low

    Finding. travel_fee grows 3.38x per planet, the same as ore value, so it costs about 1.2 band-5 ore units on every planet (60.8 at planet 1, 2.43e22 at 40).

    Impact. It is a healthy, flat sink and a good template for casing pricing (2.1).

    2. Casing as a new vertical sink

    casing_grade is the seventh track in stats.json and the horizontal intro casing_cement is planned for slice 2, but no curve exists yet.

    2.1

    casing_grade has no samples or price formula

    Medium

    Problem. Without a curve, casing cannot be balanced or sampled alongside the other six tracks.

    Suggested change. Price casing per metre as a fixed fraction of band ore value (like the travel fee), with grade levels gating which collapse pressure it holds.

    Problem. The build fails its pacing gate: slice 2 ends at 68.3 minutes, 22-62 minutes short.

    Impact. At the current 550 per minute income, landing at 90 / 110 / 130 minutes means casing and other new sinks take about 25% / 39% / 48% of money earned.

    Suggested change. Tune the casing fraction toward the 110-minute midpoint first, then re-run the bot; collapse risk will also slow mining, so expect to need less.
